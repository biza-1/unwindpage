UnwindPage Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

UnwindPage is an ebook reader for Android. It does not collect, send or share any personal data. This page explains what that means in practice.

No internet access

The app does not request the Android permission to use the internet, so it cannot connect to any server. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting services and no accounts.

Your data stays on your device

Your books, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading positions, reading history and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer has no access to them.

The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account by the app.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup file or export your notes. These files are created only when you ask for them, and they are saved where you choose. What happens to them after that is up to you.

Files and folders you choose

The app reads only the books, dictionaries and folders that you open or add yourself. It uses Android's file picker for this and does not search your storage on its own.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show progress while books are imported or a folder is scanned. You can refuse it; the app still works.
  • Background work permissions (wake lock, run after restart, foreground service, network state): added by Android's WorkManager library to run folder scans in the background. The network state permission only lets the library check whether a connection exists; it does not allow the app to use the internet.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash reports, under Google's own privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
